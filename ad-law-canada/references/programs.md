# Specialized program checks

Read this file at step 2 when the material has a gift card, a loyalty program, or telemarketing. These programs come from consumer protection laws or from specific laws. The source named them but did not go into detail.

| Program | Check | Level if it fails |
|---|---|---|
| **Gift cards** | The law requires some disclosures. It prohibits expiry of the balance and it prohibits fees. Check that the terms have no balance expiry and no fees. The source does not list the required disclosures: put them in "Not covered: lawyer". | Medium. The disclosure list: Not covered: lawyer. |
| **Loyalty programs** | Ontario and Quebec prohibit the expiry of points based on time alone. Expiry for inactivity is OK. Points that expire only because 1 year passed are prohibited by statute. | Medium if members are in Ontario or Quebec |
| **Telemarketing** | Unsolicited calls to sell a product have significant regulation. The CRTC enforces the telemarketing rules. This skill has no detail. | Not covered: lawyer |
