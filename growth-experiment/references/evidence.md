# Evidence rankings

Read this file in step 4 when a piece of evidence matches 1 of these cases.

- Repurchase (for example loyalty members who repurchase faster) is the strongest.
- Add to cart is strong but not purchase. Cart abandonment is usually high, often 70% or more.
- More signups with a flat upgrade rate is compelling, but wallets did not move. Push further.
- A return rate is purchase-level evidence in the wrong direction. Investigate it.
- 5 of 5 interviews that say "prices feel high" is middling evidence. It shows that a problem exists, not what works.
- A survey with a low response rate is weak. People rush and it costs them nothing. Build a hypothesis from a pattern, then find stronger evidence.
